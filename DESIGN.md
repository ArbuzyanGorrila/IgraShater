# CraftMine visual direction

Pixel mine with a clear orthographic grid. During gameplay the mine is centered across the full stage with a compact HUD above and contextual hints below; the right expedition panel is shown only in menus, pause and results. Source art is Kenney Pixel Platformer, rendered with point filtering; no pre-existing sprites are used. Hearts remain red, copper amber, iron cool gray, gold yellow, diamonds cyan.

Use midnight navy (#111822) as the cave background, slate (#202D3B) for UI surfaces, warm white (#F0E9DC) for text, muted blue-gray (#A7B7C8) for secondary text and amber (#F5BA63) for active controls. UI corners are square. Large readable Russian lettering, distinct button focus and disabled states. Show a directional mining preview and animate each accepted action with one short step/dig beat. No decorative interface motion during puzzle analysis.

Amber marks the primary action or an enabled danger overlay. Secondary buttons use blue-gray surfaces; restarting uses muted red. A warm-white outline marks keyboard focus. Disabled controls retain readable muted captions, skip keyboard navigation, and never dispatch actions. Tab/Shift+Tab cycles buttons; arrows navigate menus spatially and remain movement controls on the board. Only GameUI submits keyboard actions; EventSystem navigation/submit events are disabled.

The fixed 1600×900 composition and board camera use InterfaceLayout's shared letterbox scale and separate gameplay/menu viewports. The gameplay footer keeps objective/trail rules and controls separate from temporary notifications. Ore totals in pause and menus are separate rows so their baselines stay aligned with icons. Settings show the selected volume step and can return to a paused attempt, including after visiting the guide.

Restarting or leaving an unbanked attempt after a move opens a blocking confirmation showing pending XP and ore. Its default focus is “Остаться”; Esc restores the previous screen and focus. The timer and board input stop while it is open. Saved victories remain protected from duplicate banking, and unsaved victories only offer saving again until it succeeds.

Main menu: game title, playable mine vignette and Start / Rooms / Workshop / Guide / Sound. Gameplay: room title, counters and a compact character summary above the centered grid, contextual instructions beneath. Pause exposes inventory, undo, danger overlay, restart, guide and settings. Results protect focus because attempt rewards are committed there. Settings and workshop are full panel views rather than stacked modal cards.

Menu entrances use a 180 ms ease-out fade with a 10 px settle; confirmations fade in over 140 ms. Buttons respond to hover, focus and press with a subtle scale change; XP bars ease to their new value. Motion uses unscaled time, settles completely and never moves the board or delays gameplay input. Rebuilding screen contents preserves the CanvasGroup root and clears all old component and focus references.

Player-facing Russian uses “Уровень” for a puzzle map and “Уровень героя” for experience progression. Use “Выбор уровня”, “Начать заново”, “Камни”, “Щиты” and “Опыт”; avoid mining jargon and unexplained XP labels. The same wording applies to menus, tutorials, level hints and notifications.

Ore readability uses both silhouette and color: a small copper nugget, square iron, round gold and a pointed diamond. HUD resource icons share the exact sprites and material used on the board. Slate stone walls, muted earth variants and dark excavated cells keep the board states distinct. A cyan corner marker identifies the hero; yellow corners show the mining footprint and red diagonal markings show possible falling paths. The mining preview never uses a heart outline. Point filtering, full source-cell slices (18 px for Pixel Platformer, 16 px for Tiny Dungeon) and camera MSAA disabled preserve crisp art; terrain variations depend only on cell coordinates so they remain stable during moves and Undo.
